# Product Research Repository

Research is decision evidence, not a requirements source by itself. Only content explicitly adopted by an approved PRD or Project Decision becomes formal downstream input.

## Categories
- `comparables/` competitive/reference products
- `market/` market/category evidence
- `users/` target-user evidence
- `ip_copyright/` preliminary IP/copyright/appearance-risk research
- `platforms/` WeChat/Douyin/platform research
- `misc/` other evidence

## Naming
`YYYY-MM-DD_<topic>_vN.md`

## Minimum Record
Research Question, Scope, Sources, Findings, Analysis, Confidence, Product Implications, Open Questions, Recommendation, and Risk Notes where needed.

Facts and inference must be separated. Record source/date/confidence/limitations. High-risk legal/compliance conclusions require professional confirmation.
